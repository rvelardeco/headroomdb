Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy covers the Headroom dB app for iPhone.

No data is collected

Headroom dB does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while it is open, to measure sound level and frequency content. Audio is processed on your iPhone as it arrives and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved measurements (date, LAeq, maximum and duration), a Mix Check reference you capture (a frequency balance, not audio) and your settings are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
