Headroom dB

Headroom dB is a sound level meter and mix check for iPhone. It shows the A-weighted sound level with Fast and Slow response, the session LAeq and maximum, a 1/3-octave spectrum, and how a mix's tonal balance compares with a typical mix or a reference track you capture.

Get help

For questions, problems or suggestions, open an issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The meter says the microphone is off. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on Headroom dB.

How accurate is it? Levels follow IEC 61672 A-weighting and Fast/Slow time weighting, and were matched to the NIOSH Sound Level Meter app on an iPhone 16 Pro Max. A phone is not a certified sound level meter: other models, cases and covered microphones can read a few dB differently.

Very quiet rooms read higher than expected. Below about 30 dBA the iPhone microphone's own noise sets the floor.

Where are saved measurements kept? Only on your iPhone. Press and hold one to delete it.
